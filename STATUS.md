## §0. The three deliverable tiers (strategic decomposition)

The deliverable splits into three tiers, all ℂ-free, in increasing difficulty.
**Tiers 1+2 ship long before tier 3** — the executable integrator needs no
splitting field at all:

1. **Executable integrator** — needs only **one simple extension per irreducible
   factor `R_i` of `R`**: factor `R` over ℚ (Berlekamp–Zassenhaus), then compute
   `gcd(p − c·q', q)` in `ℚ[x]/(R_i)` with `c` the symbolic root. **No splitting
   field, no tower, no Trager.** Output `RootSum(R_i, c ↦ c·log(gcd …))` keeps
   roots symbolic (the LRT/Rioboo point). Cleanest tier.
2. **Relative soundness** — `d/dx[LRT] = p/q` over a *given* splitting field of
   `R·q` (a parameter, not constructed); descends to a `ℚ(x)` identity by
   injectivity `ℚ(x) ↪ K(x)`. The RT-payload work (resultant=∏roots, partial
   fractions, correspondence, assembly). No construction. (= §A below.)
3. **Unconditional soundness** — *construct* the splitting field (Trager + PET)
   to discharge tier-2's existence hypothesis. Heaviest tier; only this one needs
   the splitting-field construction (= §E). Still finite, still ℂ-free.
